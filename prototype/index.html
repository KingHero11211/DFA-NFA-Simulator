Supported questions and regex syntax

Starts with / ends in / contains a literal pattern; NOT, AND, OR (AND binds before OR); even/odd, exactly/at least/at most N occurrences of a symbol; count modulo M = R; length constraints; nonempty binary numbers divisible by N. Declare another alphabet with over {a,b}. Counts 0–40; construction limit 128 states. Small Hinglish forms such as binary strings jo 01 se end hoti hain also work.

Regex mode matches the whole string. Use (0|1)*01, a*b, (ab|ba)+, [01], ?, . (any chosen alphabet symbol), ε (empty string), or ∅ (empty language). Union is |; postfix + means one or more. No lookaround, backreferences, shorthand classes or {N} repetition.

This is a supported-language parser, not unrestricted AI. It refuses unrecognized wording. Check the interpreted language before using the generated machine as your answer.
